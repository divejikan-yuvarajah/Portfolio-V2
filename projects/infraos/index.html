01 · CONTEXT

The problem

Infrastructure works can be planned by separate organizations, making it difficult to spot schedule conflicts or see a shared status. INFRAOS is a project concept for coordinating that information; it is not an official government system or an operating national service.

Project goals

  • Explore a shared view of infrastructure project status and schedules.
  • Make cross-agency coordination and potential work conflicts visible.
  • Consider mapping and document-risk information as future product areas.
