01 · CONTEXT

The problem

The owner-provided brief describes medical records spread across uploaded documents and patient history as the design problem. The proposed prototype explores making those records easier to review and query; this is not a claim that it improves clinical outcomes.

Project goals

  • Explore extraction of structured information from uploaded medical documents.
  • Make record-grounded questions easier to ask and review with source references.
  • Keep prototype output clearly separate from diagnosis or clinician decision-making.
